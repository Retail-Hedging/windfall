How Windfall works

Windfall is a non-custodial vault. Your USDC goes from your wallet into a smart contract; the contract lends it on Aave, one of the largest lending markets in crypto. The interest is swapped into the shared PoolTogether prize pool, and every day the protocol draws winners at random. More on deposit, more entries.

  1. Open an accountA passkey creates a wallet in your browser in about 30 seconds.
  2. Add USDCBuy with a debit card inside the wallet, or send from an exchange, on the Base network.
  3. DepositYour balance appears in your account immediately and starts earning entries.
  4. Win or withdrawPrizes are paid to your account automatically. Take your money out whenever you like.

What it costs

Nothing up front. Windfall keeps 10% of the interest the vault earns; the other 90% goes into the prize pool. You never pay a fee out of your deposit, and there are no withdrawal fees beyond the network's transaction cost — a fraction of a cent on Base.

What could go wrong

This is not a bank account and is not insured. Your money sits in audited smart contracts (PoolTogether, Aave) and in USDC, a dollar-backed stablecoin. A bug in those contracts, a failure of USDC, or a problem with the Base network could result in loss. Deposit only what you can afford to lose. Full risk notes.
