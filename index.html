How it works

Deposit USDC into the Windfall vault.

Your wallet sends USDC straight to an audited PoolTogether prize vault. We never touch it.

The vault lends your USDC.

Deposits earn yield in Aave on Base. That yield — not your principal — flows into the prize pool.

Weekly draws pick winners.

Chainlink VRF randomness. Your odds scale with your balance. Winners' prizes land in their wallet automatically.

Withdraw anytime.

Burn your vault shares, get your USDC back. No lockups, no penalties.

FAQ

Is my deposit guaranteed?

No. Nothing here is guaranteed by anyone. Your USDC sits in a smart contract that lends to Aave. Smart-contract bugs, stablecoin depeg, or lending-protocol failure could lose funds. Only deposit what you can afford to lose.

Do I earn interest?

Not directly. All yield goes to the prize pool. Your expected value roughly equals the yield you'd have earned — it's just paid out as prizes to random depositors instead of evenly.

Who holds my money?

You do. It's a non-custodial vault. Windfall never has access to your funds. We earn a small percentage of the yield as a fee.

Is this a lottery?

It's prize-linked savings on the PoolTogether protocol. You don't buy tickets and you keep your principal. Check your local laws; not available where prohibited.
